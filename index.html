💕 About Us

Made With Love, One Sweet Treat at a Time

Welcome to Sweeter Thaan Honeyy! 🧁

Sweeter Thaan Honeyy is a home-based dessert business proudly serving Los Angeles, California. We specialize in homemade cakes, cupcakes, refreshing açaí bowls, handcrafted iced coffees, matcha, and seasonal favorites like our delicious Nieves Locas.

Every order is freshly prepared with quality ingredients and lots of love. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, surprising someone special, or simply craving something sweet, we're here to make your day a little sweeter.
